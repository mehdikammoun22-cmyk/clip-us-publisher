Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

1. Who we are

Clip_US Publisher (“the Tool”) is a personal tool operated by one individual to publish short video clips on their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Data we handle

  • TikTok authorization data: when the owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit, the Tool receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name) for the owner’s own account only. This is used to upload videos to that account.
  • Video files and captions: clips and captions created by the owner, stored on the owner’s computer.

The Tool does not collect, store or process personal data of any other TikTok user, and does not use cookies or analytics.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to upload the owner’s clips to the owner’s TikTok account. It is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

4. Storage and retention

Tokens and keys are stored locally on the owner’s computer. They are deleted when the owner revokes the Tool’s access from TikTok settings or deletes the local configuration file.

5. Your choices

The owner can revoke the Tool’s access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. The latest version is always available on this page.
