Clip_US Publisher

Clip_US Publisher is a personal tool used by one individual to upload short vertical video clips to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The owner edits short clips (20–60 seconds) from long videos, only from creators who have given permission.
  2. The owner signs in with TikTok Login Kit and approves access to their own account.
  3. The tool uploads each finished clip to that account through the TikTok Content Posting API, with a caption that credits the original creator.

The tool is not offered to the public, does not post on behalf of any other user, and does not collect data from other users.
